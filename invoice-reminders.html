How it works

A fixed reminder schedule, with a person for the hard cases

You choose the tone and the schedule. The tool follows it for every open invoice. When a customer disputes an invoice or asks for a payment plan, the tool stops and passes the case to you.

  1. Day −3

    Friendly heads-up

    A short note with the amount, the due date and a pay link.

  2. Day +1

    First reminder

    A polite message the day after the due date.

  3. Day +7

    Ask for a date

    The tool asks when payment will come and records the answer.

  4. Day +14

    Firmer reminder

    A clear message that refers to your payment terms.

  5. Day +21

    Hand over to you

    You get a short summary of the full history, so one call is enough.

What is different

  • Messages go from your own email address and WhatsApp number.

Questions

Common questions

Is this a debt collection agency?

No. We supply software. You stay the sender of every message, and your customers pay you directly. We do not take a percentage of the money that you collect.

Will it damage my customer relationships?

You approve the wording and the schedule before anything goes out. The tone stays polite. Disputes and payment plans go to you, not to the tool.

How do you get my invoice data?

During the pilot, we can start from a simple export (Excel or CSV) from your accounting software. Direct links to Xero and AutoCount are on our plan.

What happens to my customers' data?

We use it only to send your reminders, and we do not sell or share it. We follow Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act.

What does the pilot cost?

We agree simple pilot terms with you before we start. There is never a fee based on a percentage of collected cash.
