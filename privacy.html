How we use your data

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are

Banyan Foundry is a small studio run by John Chan in Singapore. For questions about your data, write to john@banyanfoundry.com.

Why we may have contacted you

We write to people who work at newly licensed payment and lending firms. We ask one question about their work with fraud and credit risk. We do this to learn about these problems. We do not sell anything in these messages.

What data we keep

  • Your firm's name and its entry on a public licence register.
  • Your name, role and work email, from public sources such as your firm's website. For a general inbox, we keep only the address that your firm published.
  • Your reply, if you send one.

We do not buy contact lists. We do not use tracking pixels or link tracking in these messages.

Legal basis

We rely on legitimate interest: to contact a business person about their professional work, in a small number of short messages. You can object at any time.

How long we keep it

If you do not reply, we delete your data after 6 months. If you reply, we keep your reply only while we are in contact.

Who can see it

Only John Chan. Our email provider stores the messages for us. We do not sell or share your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to correct it or to delete it. To stop all messages, reply "no" to any email from us, or write to the address above. We record your request at once and do not contact you again.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to your data protection regulator, for example the ICO in the UK, the OAIC in Australia or the ODPC in Kenya.
