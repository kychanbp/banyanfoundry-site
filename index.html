How I learn

One question at a time

  1. Step 1

Who writes this

John Chan

Singapore Writing on risk

John has spent over 10 years in fraud and credit risk at digital lenders and payment businesses, across Southeast Asia and Latin America. His work covers underwriting, anti-fraud, collections and the management of large loan books.

Earlier, he built the first credit scorecard and data warehouse at FundPark, a Hong Kong lender to small businesses. He uses Banyan Foundry to learn how new firms meet these risks, and to write down what he learns.
