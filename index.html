How we work

One problem at a time, tested with real businesses

  1. Step 1

    Find a costly task

    We look for work that owners do by hand every week and that costs them cash or time.

  2. Step 2

    Build a small tool

    We build the smallest tool that removes that task. It runs on the systems you already use.

  3. Step 3

    Pilot with a few firms

    We test it with a small group of businesses and measure the result in money and hours.

  4. Step 4

    Keep what works

    If a tool does not help, we stop it. If it helps, we make it a product.
